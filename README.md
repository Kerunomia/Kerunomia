# keru

i'm 17. i live in Kyzyl, a small city in Tuva — southern siberia, near the geographic center of asia and far from basically everything else. that's mostly the point.

i taught myself C and linux graphics, because there's nobody here to teach it and the internet doesn't sleep. mostly wayland compositors now. it's a simple relationship: the program compiles or it doesn't, and either way it never asks how i'm doing.

right now that's **[vwl](https://github.com/Kerunomia/vwl)** — a tiling wayland compositor built on wlroots. infinite canvas, animations, a lua config so i don't have to recompile just to change my mind. it works, which is more than i can say for most things.

i like software small enough to hold in my head. so the projects stay small and the nights stay long — it's dark by four in the winter here, so the sun isn't really a loss.

`C` · `Lua` · `Wayland` · `wlroots` · `Xwayland` · `Linux`
